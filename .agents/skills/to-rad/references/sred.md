# Audit-grade RADs (SR&ED and similar R&D tax credits)

Where a project claims R&D tax credits, a RAD is evidence as well as a
record. The four-part shape still holds; these are the additional demands
an assessor makes of it.

**Claim the uncertainty explicitly.** The programme funds work whose
outcome was genuinely unknown at the outset. The Question section must say
what was uncertain and why existing knowledge did not settle it — not
"we needed to pick a queue", but "we could not determine whether X would
hold under Y without attempting it".

**Show the systematic investigation.** The Trail is what distinguishes
qualifying work from ordinary engineering: a hypothesis, what was tried,
what was observed, what was concluded, and what was tried next. A trail
that reads as one option chosen on preference does not qualify. Record the
iterations, including the ones that failed — a failed attempt is stronger
evidence than a success.

**Date and attribute.** Each entry carries dates and who did the work.
Reconstructed-after-the-fact records are worth far less; write as you go.

**Keep the evidence verifiable.** Point at commits, benchmark output,
issue IDs, external sources. An assertion nobody can check is not
evidence. Where a measurement was taken, state the conditions.

**Separate the technological from the commercial.** Cost, schedule and
market reasoning belong in the RAD only where they constrained the
technical work. An assessor reads commercial justification as a signal
that the work was routine.

## Additional sections

Add these to the standard Question / Trail / Findings / Recommendation:

- **Technological uncertainty** — what was unknown, and why the existing
  knowledge base did not answer it.
- **Hypotheses and iterations** — dated, each with what was tried and what
  it showed.
- **Validation** — how the conclusion was checked, with verifiable
  references.
- **Personnel and period** — who, and over what dates.

This document describes documentation practice, not eligibility. Whether
specific work qualifies is a question for whoever prepares the claim.
