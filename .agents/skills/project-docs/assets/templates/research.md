## Question

What we needed to find out, and why it came up.

## Trail

The back-and-forth: options compared, sources consulted, what was refuted
and why, dead ends walked far enough to rule out. Keep enough that a
future reader trusts the conclusion.

Where a proof of concept was built to settle something, say what was built,
where it lives (or that it has been deleted), and whether it still runs.

## Findings

What turned out to be true. Separate the measured from the assumed - a
reader needs to know which claims to re-verify when the versions move.

## Recommendation

What to do about it, and what would change the answer. If this produced a
decision, link the ADR.
